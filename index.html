5. Pricing & Payment Terms

The amount payable for a project is set by Velnox Labs and shown on your project dashboard once confirmed. Payments are accepted via UPI to our official UPI ID, shown at the time of payment. After paying, you must submit the UTR / transaction reference number through the portal for verification. 6. Refund Policy

6.1 Requesting a refund. If you wish to request a refund for a payment already made, you may raise a refund request from your project dashboard, providing your UPI ID for the refund to be sent to.

6.2 Processing fee. Refunds are subject to a deduction of 10% of the paid amount towards payment gateway / merchant processing charges that Velnox Labs incurs and cannot recover. The remaining 90% of the amount paid is refunded to you. The exact amount to be refunded will be clearly shown to you before you confirm your refund request.

6.3 Approval process. Every refund request is reviewed by our team before processing. Once approved, the refund is sent to the UPI ID you provided, and the UTR / transaction reference number for the refund payment is recorded and shown to you on a dedicated refund status page in your dashboard, along with a final "Refund completed" confirmation once the transfer is made.

6.4 Timelines. Refund requests are typically reviewed within 3–5 business days of submission, and processed within a further 3–5 business days of approval. Timelines may vary depending on banking/UPI processing times outside our control.

6.5 Non-refundable situations. Amounts paid for work that has already been completed and delivered, or delivered milestones that the client has approved, are generally not eligible for a refund. Refund eligibility for partially completed work is assessed case by case by our team.
